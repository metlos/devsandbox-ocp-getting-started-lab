= Step 2.1: Switch to your clean project
:source-highlighter: highlightjs

.Using the Web Console
====
In the left navigation, expand *console:qs-nav-home[Home]*, select *resource:project.openshift.io/v1/Project[Projects]*,
then choose the resource:project.openshift.io/v1/Project[*`{lab-namespace}`*,scope=cluster,name={lab-namespace}] project.
