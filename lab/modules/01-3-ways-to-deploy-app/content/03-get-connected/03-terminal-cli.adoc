= Terminal (CLI)
:source-highlighter: highlightjs

Expand the console:qs-masthead-usermenu[top-right menu] showing your username, then click "https://oauth-openshift.{openshift-cluster-ingress-domain}/oauth/token/request[Copy login command]".
Click on "Display token" then copy the `oc login` command.

Open a terminal session on your machine and paste the `oc login` command


[source,role="execute",subs="attributes+"]
----
oc login --token=... --server={openshift-api-url}
----

NOTE: If `oc` is not already installed on your machine, you can download it directly from the OpenShift console: 
click the *console:qs-masthead-help[?]* icon in the top-right corner, select link:{openshift-console-url}/command-line-tools[*Command Line Tools*], and download the version of `oc` 
that matches your operating system.
